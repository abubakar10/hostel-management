Resident (Client) Portal

Page 2 of 3

Who is this for?

Students and hostel residents who live in the hostel. They log in with their Student ID or email and access a personal dashboard tailored to their stay.

Dashboard & Profile

Personal Dashboard

At-a-glance view of pending fees, attendance rate, active complaints, and pending leave requests.

Quick Actions

One-click shortcuts to submit a complaint, apply for leave, request a room transfer, or view profile.

My Profile

View personal details — name, ID, contact, course, room assignment, hostel name, and resident status.

Notifications

Receive alerts about fee reminders, leave decisions, complaint updates, and hostel announcements.

Fees & Attendance

Fee Summary

See total, paid, and pending fee amounts at a glance.

Fee History

Full list of hostel, mess, security, and fine charges with due dates, payment dates, and status.

Attendance Stats

View total days, present/absent counts, and overall attendance percentage.

Attendance Log

Filter daily records by date range — see present, absent, or late status with remarks.

Requests & Complaints

Submit Complaints

Report maintenance, cleanliness, security, or other issues with title, description, category, and priority level.

Track Complaints

Monitor status (open → in progress → resolved) and read admin resolution notes.

Apply for Leave

Request vacation, emergency, or weekend leave with dates, reason, and emergency contact.

Leave Status

See whether leave is pending, approved, or rejected — with admin remarks.

Room Transfer Request

Browse available rooms in the same hostel and submit a transfer request with a reason.

Transfer Tracking

Follow pending, approved, or rejected transfer requests in real time.

Account & Access
  • Single sign-in page — system auto-detects student vs admin
  • Forgot password via email or Student ID
  • Secure password reset with email token
  • Mobile-friendly card views and detail modals
  • Install as a PWA app on phone or desktop
  • Dark mode support for comfortable viewing

Hostel Manager Portal

Page 3 of 3

Who is this for?

Hostel wardens, administrators, and super admins who run day-to-day operations — from registering residents to collecting fees, managing staff, and generating financial reports.

Core Operations

Admin Dashboard

KPIs: students, rooms, occupancy %, revenue, pending fees, complaints, maintenance, attendance rate, trends & deadlines.

Resident Management

Add, edit, delete residents (students, job-based, short-term). Upload photos. Search and view full profiles.

Room Management

Create rooms, set capacity & type, track status (available/occupied/maintenance), allocate students to rooms.

Fee Collection

Create fees (hostel, mess, security, fine), auto-calculate from room type, mark paid, view receipts, send overdue reminders.

Attendance

Mark daily attendance (present/absent/late), bulk mark all present, generate monthly reports.

Visitor Log

Check-in visitors with ID & purpose, link to resident, check-out, search and filter active visitors.

Services & Maintenance

Leave Management

Review, approve, or reject student leave requests. Create leave on behalf of residents.

Mess Management

Daily menu (breakfast/lunch/dinner), meal attendance tracking, mess fee status & payment.

Complaints

Manage all complaints — assign staff, set priority, update status, add resolution notes.

Maintenance

Create work orders, assign staff, track status, record cost and completion date.

Room Transfers

Approve or reject transfer requests. Auto-update room occupancy on approval.

Notifications

View, mark read, and manage system alerts. Send bulk fee reminders to overdue students.

Administration & Reports

Staff Directory

Manage wardens, cleaners, security, chefs, finance officers, and more — with roles, shifts & salary.

Inventory

Track hostel assets & supplies — quantity, location, condition, purchase info, and supplier details.

Documents

Upload & manage resident documents (ID cards, contracts, photos) — filter, download, delete.

Financial Reports

Monthly income & expenses, profit & loss charts, category breakdowns, year/month filters.

Multi-Hostel (Super Admin)

Manage multiple hostels, create admin users, assign roles, switch hostel context from header.

Theme & PWA

Dark/light mode toggle. Installable progressive web app for desktop and mobile use.
